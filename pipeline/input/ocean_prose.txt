The ocean is beautiful at sunset when the colors turn orange and pink and purple. The waves crash on the shore like thunder and I feel peaceful listening to them. The sea is vast and deep and holds secrets in its darkness. Sometimes I walk along the beach alone and think about my life and what it all means. The salt air fills my lungs and the wind touches my face and for a moment everything is still. The ocean does not judge it simply moves. It carries heavy things but it keeps going. I want to be like the ocean calm on the surface but deep underneath. The tides come and go like the days of our lives. What stays is the water what changes is the light. At dawn the ocean is silver and quiet. At noon it is blue and bright. At sunset it is fire. At night it is mystery. The ocean teaches me that beauty is not always loud sometimes it is the quiet rhythm of waves on sand. The moon pulls the tides and the tides pull my heart. I am small beside the sea but I am part of it. Every drop of water in the ocean was once a cloud and every cloud was once a wave. Everything returns to the sea eventually.
